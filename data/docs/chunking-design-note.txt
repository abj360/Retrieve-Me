Design Note — Clause-Aware Chunking

Fixed-size token windows split legal clauses mid-sentence, which destroys
exact-match recall for clause lookups. A clause-aware splitter keeps sentence
and clause boundaries intact and packs text up to the token budget instead.

Overlapping consecutive chunks by a sentence or two preserves context across
boundaries without doubling the index size.

Clause references (Section 3.1, Article 7) are extracted into chunk
metadata so exact-clause queries can be boosted at fusion time later.
